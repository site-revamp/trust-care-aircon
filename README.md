# TrustCare Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

TrustCare Aircon provides aircon maintenance, servicing, cleaning, repairs, and installation for residential and commercial customers in Singapore. The homepage leads with TrustCare’s diagnosis-first approach and helps visitors compare services before making an enquiry.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make servicing prices easier to compare.** The homepage groups one-time servicing and quarterly contracts by unit count, lists gas top-up prices by refrigerant, and directs chemical overhaul customers to request a quote. The price guide flags contract billing, visit counts, inclusions, GST, and additional charges for confirmation.
2. **Explain how TrustCare recommends work.** The homepage turns its diagnosis-first message into three steps: assess the unit, explain the findings, and confirm the scope and charges before proceeding. Separate chemical wash and overhaul descriptions help visitors understand why a technician might recommend each option. Project photos and testimonials are placeholders awaiting genuine examples and verified reviews.
3. **Make contact options easier to find.** A dedicated contact section groups the phone, WhatsApp, email, address, and published hours. A mobile Call and WhatsApp bar keeps both options within reach, and the enquiry form opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message. Confirm the legal business name and UEN, contact details, appointment availability, and warranty terms before publication.

## Design Improvements

Consistent service and pricing cards help visitors scan options. The blue and teal palette ties together the price guide, the three-step assessment section, and the enquiry actions.
