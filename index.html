What servicing involves

Know What Happens During a Service.

A clearer explanation of routine maintenance helps customers understand what is included and when additional work may be needed.

1
Initial inspection

Check the system's visible condition and reported symptoms.

2
Filter and accessible component cleaning

Remove dust and buildup from components covered by the agreed service scope.

3
Drainage inspection

Check drainage and address accessible blockages where included.

4
Operational checks

Check the unit's operation and discuss any concerns observed.

5
Recommendations

Explain any further work suggested and its applicable cost before proceeding.
